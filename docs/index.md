# eCAL

Analytical estimation of the energy cost of the AI lifecycle (J/bit).

eCAL computes the total energy consumed across the full AI model lifecycle —
data transmission, preprocessing, training, evaluation, and inference — using
closed-form FLOP formulas and hardware power profiles.

Published in **IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications (JSAC), 2026**.
